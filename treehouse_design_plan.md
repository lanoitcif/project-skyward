# Project Skyward: Treehouse Architectural Design Plan

## 1. Site Constraints & Specifications
- **Central Support:** Single mature Eastern White Pine (*Pinus strobus*), ~24-30" diam.
- **Available Ground Area:** Approx. 19x19 ft of stable, flat terrain visible in the lidar scans.
- **Elevation Points:** Existing rigging visible in site images indicates primary deck levels could be situated at ~8ft and ~12ft above grade.

## 2. Structural Foundation
Since the tree is a single robust trunk rather than a multi-stem cluster:
- **Primary Support (The Hub):** Heavy-duty Treehouse Attachment Bolts (TABs) will be installed directly into the main trunk. This will support the main load-bearing beams close to the center of gravity without girdling the tree.
- **Secondary Support (Knee Braces / Ground Posts):** To stabilize the 19x19 ft deck area and prevent excessive torque on the trunk during high winds, thick 4x4 or 6x6 knee braces will angle away from the trunk. For the outer perimeter, ground-supported posts set in concrete footings will anchor the extended deck to the flat ground.

## 3. Deck Layout & Framing
- **Footprint:** The deck will be framed in roughly an octagonal or rectangular shape wrapping entirely around the central trunk. Dimensions are adjustable and should be evaluated via the included `viewer` tool.
- **Clearance:** A minimum of 3 inches of clearance must be left between the decking/framing and the tree bark to allow for seasonal growth and natural trunk sway.
- **Framing Materials:** Pressure-treated 2x10s for the main supporting beams and 2x8s for the floor joists. Decking can be composite or cedar for longevity against heavy pine needle fall.

## 4. Superstructure & Access
- **Lower Level (8ft):** The primary entrance and wrap-around viewing deck. Access via a secure wooden staircase curving around the base.
- **Upper Cabin (12ft):** A smaller enclosed structure (the "house") built on the main platform. It will feature a steep, sloped roof to ensure thick pine needles shed easily to the ground without accumulating on the roof.
- **Railings:** 36-inch high sturdy wooden railings with vertical balusters spaced no more than 4 inches apart to meet general safety standards.
